Privacy Policy

History Teller · Last updated: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application History Teller (the “App”), developed by Decima (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”), handles information. We designed the App to work without collecting any personal information about you.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The App has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. The App does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or device identifiers.

The only information the App sends to us is a set of anonymous counters that record how far players get in the game. They contain nothing about you. See Anonymous progress counters below.

Data stored on your device

The App saves the following data locally on your device only, so the game can function. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us:

  • Your game progress (which levels and chapters you have completed).
  • Your preferences (music, sound effects, haptics, and language).

Deleting the App removes this data.

Anonymous progress counters

So that we can tell whether the chapters we write are being played and where players stop, the App sends us a short message the first time you open a chapter and the first time you complete an episode. The message is a plain label such as done:rome:sabines, meaning “someone finished the episode The Sabine Women in the chapter Ancient Rome”. Each label is sent at most once per installation.

On our server we keep only a running total for each label — a single number saying how many times it has arrived. We do not store an identifier for you or your device, a date or time, or your IP address, and the message itself carries none of those. Because nothing is kept alongside the number, these messages cannot be linked to you, cannot be linked to one another, and cannot be linked to a purchase. They cannot be used to build a profile or to track you across apps or websites.

The App also asks our server for these totals, as percentages, to show how many players solved each episode.

Purchases and subscriptions

The App offers optional one-time chapter purchases and auto-renewable subscriptions. All payments and subscriptions are processed by the store you installed the App from — Apple through the App Store, or Google through Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies, available at apple.com/legal/privacy and policies.google.com/privacy. To restore previous purchases, the App asks the store to verify your existing entitlements; this is handled entirely by that store.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. The anonymous counters described above contain no personal information and cannot identify anyone, so the App is suitable for a general audience.

Third parties

We do not share personal information with third parties, because we do not collect any. Two third parties are involved in running the App:

  • Apple or Google, solely for processing purchases made through their stores, as described above.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts the game’s chapters, artwork and the anonymous counters, and therefore handles the network requests the App makes to us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
