Privacy Policy

History Teller · Last updated: July 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application History Teller (the “App”), developed by Decima (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”), handles information. We designed the App to work without collecting any personal information about you.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The App has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. The App does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or device identifiers.

Data stored on your device

The App saves the following data locally on your device only, so the game can function. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us:

  • Your game progress (which levels and chapters you have completed).
  • Your preferences (music, sound effects, haptics, and language).

Deleting the App removes this data.

Purchases and subscriptions

The App offers optional one-time chapter purchases and auto-renewable subscriptions. All payments and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple’s handling of that information is governed by Apple’s privacy policy, available at apple.com/legal/privacy. To restore previous purchases, the App asks Apple to verify your existing entitlements; this is handled entirely by Apple.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Because we collect no data at all, the App is suitable for a general audience.

Third parties

We do not share any information with third parties, because we do not collect any. The only third party involved is Apple, solely for processing App Store purchases as described above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
