Privacy Policy

History Teller · Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application History Teller (the “App”), developed by Decima (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”), handles information. The App has no user accounts and no sign-in, and we never ask for your name, email or other contact details.

Advertising

The App can show optional rewarded video ads: if you choose to watch one, it unlocks one level of a paid chapter. Ads are never shown unless you tap to watch one. Ads are served by Google AdMob and the advertising partners it works with. To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google and its partners may collect:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; Google Advertising ID on Android);
  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model, operating system, language and time zone;
  • information about the ads you see and interact with.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before using your device data for personalized ads, using Google’s consent form. If you decline, ads may still be shown on request, but they will not be personalized. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Install and purchase measurement

We use Tenjin to understand how players find the App and whether our advertising campaigns work. Tenjin receives app installs and launches, the source of an install (for example, the ad campaign that led to it), purchases made in the App (product, price, currency and transaction ID, so that the store can verify them) and revenue from ads shown. This may include your advertising identifier, IP address and device information. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland and decline consent in the App, no data is sent to Tenjin. Tenjin’s privacy policy: tenjin.com/privacy.

Your choices

  • In the App: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, open Settings → Privacy choices at any time to give or withdraw consent.
  • On iOS: the App asks for permission to track through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change it in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).

Data stored on your device

The App saves the following data locally on your device so the game can function. This data is not sent to us:

  • Your game progress (which levels and chapters you have completed, and levels unlocked by watching an ad).
  • Your preferences (music, sound effects, haptics, language) and your privacy choice.

Deleting the App removes this data.

Purchases and subscriptions

The App offers optional one-time chapter purchases and auto-renewable subscriptions. All payments and subscriptions are processed by the store you installed the App from — Apple through the App Store, or Google through Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies, available at apple.com/legal/privacy and policies.google.com/privacy. To restore previous purchases, the App asks the store to verify your existing entitlements; this is handled entirely by that store.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in the European Economic Area), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

Third parties

  • Google (AdMob) — rewarded video ads and the consent form, as described above.
  • Tenjin — install, purchase and ad revenue measurement, as described above.
  • Apple or Google — processing purchases made through their stores.
  • Cloudflare — hosting the game’s chapters and artwork, and therefore serving the download requests the App makes. We do not use it to collect anything about you.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Sharing device data with advertising and measurement partners, as described above, may count as “selling” or “sharing” under some U.S. state laws. To opt out, limit ad tracking on your device (see “Your choices”) or contact us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not hold an account for you, the data described above sits with Google and Tenjin; to exercise these rights, contact us and include your device’s advertising identifier if you can — we will pass the request on. You may also contact Google and Tenjin directly, or complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.

Contact
